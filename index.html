The name is Sophia :)

Design is my language, storytelling my craft.

I’m an artist and designer who brings curiosity, energy, and thoughtful thinking to every project. With over five years of experience, primarily in in-house design, I’ve worked across everything from corporate campaigns to helping new businesses build their visual identities. I’m drawn to minimal design, thoughtful details, and the little decisions that make a big difference.

I’ve always been a maker. My iPad is filled with ideas, drawings, and notes that evolve as I revisit and refine them. That same spirit of exploration carries into my design process: I experiment, reflect, and iterate, building on each idea with intention. I believe good design goes beyond aesthetics. It’s about understanding people, telling meaningful stories, and creating experiences that connect with their needs.
